## Overview
This is a **Spider Plate** — a lightweight, symmetrical mechanical component with a central hub and multiple radial arms (spokes) extending outward, giving it a spider-like appearance.

It is commonly used in:
- Wheel hubs
- Couplings
- Rotating fixtures
- Mounting plates
- Custom machinery where good strength-to-weight ratio and balanced load distribution are needed.

## Visualization / What it looks like
The part features:
- A thick circular central hub with a bore (hole) in the middle for shaft mounting or bolting.
- 6 or 8 equally spaced radial arms (spokes) extending from the center to the outer ring.
- Multiple mounting holes or clearance holes drilled on the arms and the outer perimeter for easy assembly.
- Fillets and chamfers on edges for better strength and manufacturability.
- Overall circular shape with material removed between the arms to reduce weight while keeping rigidity.

It has a clean, professional machined look — symmetric, balanced, and ready for CNC machining or 3D printing.

(When you open the file in SolidWorks, you will see a nice revolved base with extruded cuts and circular patterns for the arms and holes.)

## Features Used in Modeling
- Revolved Boss/Base for the main circular body
- Extruded Cut for the central bore and arm profiles
